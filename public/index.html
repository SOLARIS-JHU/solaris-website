  1. Learning to Control PDEs with Differentiable Predictive Control and Time-Integrated Neural Operators

    Dibakar, Ján, and Somdatta

  2. SCORE: Statistical Certification of Regions of Attraction via Extreme Value Theory

    Pietro, Panos, and Ján

  3. Fixed-time-stable ODE Representation of Lasso

    Liang, Yunhong, Wallace, Efstathios, Richard, and Ján

    Also accepted as an IEEE Control Systems Letters paper.
  4. Stability Enhanced Gaussian Process Variational Autoencoders

  6. Safe Receding Horizon Mixed-Integer Differentiable Predictive Control for Degradation-Aware Battery Dispatch

    Eshagh, Ján, and Ben

  7. Koopman Meets Discrete-Time Control Barrier Functions: A Linear Model Predictive Control Framework

  8. Polynomial Parametric Koopman Operators for Stochastic MPC

    Efstathios, Wallace, Liang, Ján, and Richard
